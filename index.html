    HOW IT WORKS

    A plan for your NX workflow.

    1. Configure

      Choose assembly scope, exclusions, geometry preparation and export formats.

    2. Review

      Check the summary, planned filenames and expected report. Counts stay pending until NX processes your assembly.

    3. Download

      Save the JSON configuration or a readable report. This site has no connection to NX; a compatible NX journal or utility is needed to apply the plan and produce STEP or Parasolid files.

    The workflow targets unassociated geometry in the existing part. It does not request a new part or source modification. A single body and opening closure are targets, not guaranteed outcomes.
